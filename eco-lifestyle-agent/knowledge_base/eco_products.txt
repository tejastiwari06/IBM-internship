CATEGORY: Kitchen & Food Storage
----------------------------------
Product: Beeswax Wraps
Description: Reusable food wrap made from organic cotton coated with beeswax, jojoba oil, and tree resin. Replaces plastic cling wrap.
Usage: Wrap sandwiches, cover bowls, store vegetables. Lasts 1+ year with proper care.
Brands to look for: Bee's Wrap, Abeego, Khala & Co.
Impact: Replaces up to 200 sheets of plastic wrap per wrap.

Product: Stainless Steel Water Bottle
Description: Insulated double-wall stainless steel bottle that keeps drinks cold for 24 hours and hot for 12 hours.
Usage: Daily hydration, gym, travel, office.
Brands to look for: Hydro Flask, S'well, Klean Kanteen, MIRA.
Impact: Replaces ~156 single-use plastic bottles per year per person.

Product: Glass Food Storage Containers
Description: Borosilicate glass containers with airtight lids, safe for oven, microwave, and dishwasher.
Usage: Meal prep, leftovers, bulk food storage.
Brands to look for: Pyrex, OXO, Snapware.
Impact: Avoids plastic leaching into food and eliminates need for disposable containers.

Product: Bamboo Cutting Boards
Description: Cutting boards made from fast-growing bamboo — naturally antimicrobial and sustainable.
Usage: Chopping vegetables, fruits, and bread.
Impact: Bamboo grows 30x faster than hardwood trees.

Product: Compost Bin (Countertop)
Description: Small countertop bin for collecting food scraps before transferring to outdoor compost.
Brands to look for: OXO Good Grips, Full Circle, Bamboozle.
Usage: Collect vegetable peels, coffee grounds, eggshells for composting.
