INDIA - CENTRAL GOVERNMENT SCHEMES
-------------------------------------

Scheme: National Solar Mission (Jawaharlal Nehru National Solar Mission - JNNSM)
Description: India's flagship program to promote solar energy production and use.
Benefits for citizens:
  - Subsidies available for rooftop solar panel installation (up to 40% for 1-3 kW systems).
  - Net metering policy allows households to sell surplus solar electricity back to the grid.
  - PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana: 300 units of free electricity per month for solar adopters.
How to apply: Visit pmsuryaghar.gov.in or contact local electricity distribution company (DISCOM).
Impact: India aims for 500 GW of renewable energy by 2030.

Scheme: UJALA Scheme (Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs for All)
Description: Distributes LED bulbs at subsidized rates to replace inefficient incandescent bulbs.
Benefits: LED bulbs available at Rs. 70–120 vs. market price of Rs. 250+. 
How to apply: Contact your electricity distribution company; show your electricity bill and ID.
Impact: UJALA has distributed 367+ million LED bulbs, saving 47 billion units of electricity annually.

Scheme: FAME India (Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles)
Description: Subsidizes purchase of electric vehicles (EVs) — two-wheelers, three-wheelers, four-wheelers, and buses.
Phase II Benefits:
  - Subsidy of Rs. 10,000 per kWh on EV battery for two-wheelers.
  - Subsidies for electric buses for public transport.
  - Support for charging infrastructure development.
How to apply: Subsidy applied at point of purchase from registered EV dealers.
Website: fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in

Scheme: National Biogas and Manure Management Programme (NBMMP)
Description: Provides subsidies for setting up biogas plants at household and community levels.
Benefits: Converts organic waste (kitchen scraps, cow dung) into clean cooking fuel and bio-slurry fertilizer.
Subsidy: Central subsidy of Rs. 7,000–18,000 per biogas plant depending on size and region.
How to apply: Apply through Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) or state nodal agency.

Scheme: Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM)
Description: National cleanliness and waste management initiative.
Solid Waste Management Component:
  - Community composting and waste segregation at source.
  - Door-to-door waste collection and processing.
  - Segregation into wet waste, dry recyclables, and domestic hazardous waste.
How citizens benefit: Free household bins for waste segregation; community compost pits in many cities.
Website: swachhbharat.mygov.in

Scheme: National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)
Description: Aims to reduce air pollution in 131 cities by 40% by 2026.
Citizen benefits: Improved air quality monitoring; awareness programs; pollution reduction measures.
Action for citizens: Use SAMEER app (System of Air Quality and Weather Forecasting and Research) to check local AQI.

INDIA - STATE-LEVEL HIGHLIGHTS
---------------------------------

Kerala - Haritha Keralam Mission:
  - Comprehensive waste management: plastic-free campaign, decentralized composting.
  - Home Composting support: Free compost bins and training for households.
  - Kudumbashree waste management units collect and process organic waste.

Karnataka - Solar Policy:
  - Rooftop solar subsidy up to 40% for residential customers.
  - BESCOM (Bangalore Electricity Supply Company) offers net metering and group net metering.

Maharashtra - Plastic Ban:
  - Ban on single-use plastics including bags, cups, plates, cutlery, straws since 2018.
  - Penalties: Rs. 5,000 for first offense; Rs. 10,000 for second; Rs. 25,000+ for third.

Delhi - EV Policy 2020:
  - Subsidy of Rs. 5,000/kWh (up to Rs. 30,000) for electric two-wheelers.
  - Waiver of road tax and registration fee for EVs.
  - Incentive for scrapping old polluting vehicles and switching to EV.

INTERNATIONAL GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS
------------------------------------

United States:
- Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) 2022: Up to $7,500 tax credit for new EVs, $4,000 for used EVs.
- Home Efficiency Rebates: Up to $8,000 for heat pumps, $1,750 for heat pump water heaters.
- Residential Clean Energy Credit: 30% tax credit for solar panels, battery storage, wind turbines.
- ENERGY STAR Program: Free energy audits; rebates on efficient appliances through utilities.
- Contact: energystar.gov for product rebates and home improvement credits.

European Union:
- EU Emissions Trading System (ETS): Carbon pricing that drives clean energy transition.
- Renovation Wave Strategy: Financial support for building insulation and energy-efficient renovations.
- European Green Deal: Aims for carbon neutrality by 2050; funds clean transport and clean energy.
- Farm to Fork Strategy: Promotes sustainable, organic food systems.

United Kingdom:
- Boiler Upgrade Scheme: £7,500 grant for replacing gas boilers with heat pumps.
- Electric Vehicle Charge Point Grant: Up to £350 off home EV charger installation.
- Great British Insulation Scheme: Free or subsidized home insulation for eligible households.
- Green Finance: Green mortgages and green bonds for energy-efficient home improvements.

Australia:
- Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme (SRES): Certificates (STCs) that reduce the upfront cost of solar panels.
- Electric Car Discount: FBT exemption for EVs priced below the luxury car tax threshold.
- Household Energy Upgrades Fund: Low-interest loans for energy-efficient home improvements.

GLOBAL CITIZEN ENGAGEMENT PROGRAMS
--------------------------------------
- UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): Citizens can pledge action at un.org/sustainabledevelopment
- WWF Earth Hour: Annual event; switch off lights for 1 hour on the last Saturday of March.
- Plastic Free July: Global challenge to refuse single-use plastics; plasticfreejuly.org
- Earth Day Network: April 22 — global campaigns, pledges, and local events; earthday.org
- Climate Reality Project: Volunteer to become a Climate Reality Leader and educate others.
- 1.5°C Pledge: Personal carbon reduction pledges aligned with Paris Agreement goals.
- GoodPlanet Foundation: Educational programs on ecology and sustainable development.

HOW TO FIND LOCAL PROGRAMS
-----------------------------
1. Search "[your city] + green rebates" or "[your state] + solar subsidy" on Google.
2. Visit your local municipal corporation website for waste management and composting programs.
3. Check your electricity company's website for LED and appliance upgrade rebates.
4. Use the Database of State Incentives for Renewables & Efficiency at dsireusa.org.
5. In India: Check rajyasabha.nic.in or pib.gov.in for the latest central government schemes.
6. Visit energysavingtrust.org.uk for UK-specific grants and support programs.
